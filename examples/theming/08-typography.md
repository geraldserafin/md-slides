## Sizes and spacing

Front matter shorthands for the most common knobs:

- `heading-size: 72` and `text-size: 28` (plain numbers are pixels)
- `spacing: 26` — the gap between text blocks
- `padding: 8vmin 12vmin` — the slide's inner margin
- `accent: #ffb454`, `heading-color`, `text-color` — colors for this slide
