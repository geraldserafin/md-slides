## Controls

- **→ Space PageDown Enter** next step · **← PageUp Backspace** previous
- **Home / End** first / last · click right 3/4 = next, left 1/4 = back · swipe on touch screens
- **o** slide drawer (thumbnails of all slides, stays open while you present) · **t** theme · **f** fullscreen · **p** presenter window · **h** help
- URL: `#12` jumps to step 12, `?theme=paper` picks a theme, `?deck=name` picks a deck
