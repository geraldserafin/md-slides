## Slide drawer and export

- **o** (or the ☰ button that appears when you move the mouse) opens the drawer: a thumbnail of every slide,
  the current one highlighted. Click to jump; **Esc** or **o** closes it
- `?print=slides` — one page per slide, `?print=steps` — one page per animation step; then *Print → Save as PDF*
- or from the command line: `slides build deck/ -t pdf [--steps]`
